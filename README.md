# RingtoneSwitcher_Windows
PC-based P2P transfer assistant

No data cable or Bluetooth pairing required! Fast two-way file transfer between computer and phone, manage your vast music library with ease ⚡💻

The perfect file transfer partner for Windows desktops and laptops!
" Ringtone Switcher - PC P2P Transfer Assistant " is specially developed for mobile apps. No iTunes installation or cloud drive login is required. Utilizing a direct LAN connection, it allows you to directly transfer massive amounts of lossless songs, videos, or folders from your computer's hard drive to your mobile phone at ultra-high speeds of tens of MB per second!

💻 Core strengths of the computer assistant:

⚡High-speed, data-free local network transfer: Connect your computer and mobile phone to the same Wi-Fi network or mobile hotspot for high-speed direct connection using pure TCP binary protocol. This does not consume any mobile data, and thousands of lossless music tracks can be synchronized within minutes.

📷Dynamic QR Code Scanning Connects Instantly: The computer opens an assistant that automatically monitors and draws a real-time QR code. Simply open the app on your phone and scan to instantly establish a connection, eliminating the tedious process of manually entering IP addresses and communication ports.

📁Mouse drag and drop and multi-level directory preservation: Supports dragging and dropping files or entire folders directly into the window, fully preserving the multi-level subdirectory structure. When transferred to the Android device, it is automatically categorized into the Music, Movies, or Pictures folders according to the format.

🔄Intelligent breakpoint resume and anti-duplicate verification: In the event of a network interruption, the system automatically remembers the byte offset at the breakpoint and resumes transmission immediately upon reconnection. Existing fully matching files are automatically skipped, saving valuable time.

📶Physical NIC Smart Priority and Custom Port: Automatically identifies physical Wi-Fi/Ethernet NICs and filters out virtual NIC interference; supports free customization of communication ports.
